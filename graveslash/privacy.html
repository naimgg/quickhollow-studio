Graveslash: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Graveslash · Quick Hollow Studio

The short version

Graveslash does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the game stores

Your progress (gold, gear, stages reached and settings) is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or choosing "Reset all progress" in Settings, deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for or collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.
  • It does not use advertising or analytics services, and contains no third-party tracking code.
  • It does not make network requests while you play. All art, sound and music ship inside the app.
  • It has no in-app purchases.

Children

Because no personal information is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles data (for example purchases or cloud saves), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.

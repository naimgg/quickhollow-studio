Read the mark. Cut the right way. Survive.

Graveslash is a one-thumb dark fantasy slasher drawn like an old woodcut print. Every enemy carries a mark that shows which way to cut. Fight through six realms and six bosses, forge your gear, and descend into the endless Abyss.

No ads, no purchases, no account. Plays offline.
